About Automated Site-Specific Pesticide Application

Automated Site-Specific Pesticide Application is an intelligent plant pathology assistant designed for Indian farmers and agronomists. Our platform uses cutting-edge deep learning models — including ResNet, EfficientNet, VGG16, MobileNet, DenseNet, and Inception — to analyze leaf images and diagnose diseases with laboratory-grade precision.

Every diagnosis is customized to your farm's geographic location, accounting for local climate, soil conditions, and regional pest pressures. We provide 4-tier treatment protocols covering alerts, organic remedies, chemical interventions, and nutritional management.

Our mission is to empower every farmer — from smallholders to large agri-businesses — with the same diagnostic intelligence that was once available only to advanced research labs.
